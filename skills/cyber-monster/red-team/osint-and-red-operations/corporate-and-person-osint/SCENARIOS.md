# Structured Case Cards and Real-World Scenarios

> Sub-skill: [`corporate-and-person-osint`](SKILL.md) | Family: **OSINT and Red Operations**

Two worked shapes for this technique. Identifiers are placeholders for the engagement's own assets; no incident below is a fabricated advisory.

## Case 1: Generated Addresses Reported as Staff

- **Surface & Preconditions:** An engagement delivers a contact list built from a single observed address format.
- **Root Cause & Impact:** The format was not confirmed, so many addresses are wrong and some belong to unrelated people.
- **Verification & Fix:** Require two independently published samples, mark the rest unverified, and deliver only verified contacts as facts.

## Case 2: Namesake Attributed as Employee

- **Surface & Preconditions:** A social profile with a matching name is recorded as the person holding a role.
- **Root Cause & Impact:** The name match has no identifier behind it, and the profile belongs to someone else.
- **Verification & Fix:** Anchor the person on a filing or a company post, and record the profile only as a candidate with the corroborating evidence required.
