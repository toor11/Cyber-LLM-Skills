# Structured Case Cards and Real-World Scenarios

> Sub-skill: [`osint-methodology-verification-and-bias`](SKILL.md) | Family: **OSINT and Red Operations**

Two worked shapes for this technique. Identifiers are placeholders for the engagement's own assets; no incident below is a fabricated advisory.

## Case 1: Four Sources, One Origin

- **Surface & Preconditions:** A draft cites four outlets for a claim about a facility.
- **Root Cause & Impact:** All four quote the same press release, so the claim has one origin and no independent confirmation.
- **Verification & Fix:** Group by origin, mark the claim single-sourced, and add a technical source such as a peering registry or certificate record.

## Case 2: Conflict Suppressed

- **Surface & Preconditions:** A facility tenant list does not include the company, and the draft omits the list entirely.
- **Root Cause & Impact:** The omitted observation is the one that weakens the claim, so the report overstates confidence.
- **Verification & Fix:** Record the conflict with its retrieval date and present both observations; let the reader see the strength of the evidence.
