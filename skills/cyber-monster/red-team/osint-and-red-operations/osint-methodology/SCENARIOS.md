# Structured Case Cards and Real-World Scenarios

> Sub-skill: [`osint-methodology`](SKILL.md) | Family: **OSINT and Red Operations**

Two worked shapes for this technique. Identifiers are placeholders for the engagement's own assets; no incident below is a fabricated advisory.

## Case 1: Report Built From Memory

- **Surface & Preconditions:** An analyst writes a conclusion about domain ownership without stored observations.
- **Root Cause & Impact:** No one can re-check the claim, and a later registrar change makes it unverifiable.
- **Verification & Fix:** Re-collect with provenance, cite the timestamped observation, and downgrade claims that no stored record supports.

## Case 2: Confirmation-Only Collection

- **Surface & Preconditions:** The plan lists sources that can only confirm the hypothesis and no source that could refute it.
- **Root Cause & Impact:** The conclusion is predetermined by the source selection, not by the evidence.
- **Verification & Fix:** Add a refuting source class to every hypothesis and record what it showed, even when it agreed.
