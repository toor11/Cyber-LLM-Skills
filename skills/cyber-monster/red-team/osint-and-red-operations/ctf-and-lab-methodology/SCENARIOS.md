# Structured Case Cards and Real-World Scenarios

> Sub-skill: [`ctf-and-lab-methodology`](SKILL.md) | Family: **OSINT and Red Operations**

Two worked shapes for this technique. Identifiers are placeholders for the engagement's own assets; no incident below is a fabricated advisory.

## Case 1: Afternoon Lost to One Approach

- **Surface & Preconditions:** An analyst spends four hours on a decompiler path that cannot work for a stripped binary.
- **Root Cause & Impact:** No time box was set, and no notebook entry recorded the dead end.
- **Verification & Fix:** Write the hypothesis, set a box, and check the format before choosing a tool; record the dead end so the next person skips it.

## Case 2: Solution That Cannot Be Replayed

- **Surface & Preconditions:** A challenge is solved and the write-up lists only the final command.
- **Root Cause & Impact:** The intermediate decisions were not recorded, so the technique cannot be reused on a similar challenge.
- **Verification & Fix:** Notebook each attempt with command, output, and decision, and publish the sequence rather than the answer.
